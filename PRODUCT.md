# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Veterinarians and farm staff at DSP Farm Bandung. They use VetPro on a phone, standing in a pen or beside a cage, often in bright daylight, sometimes one-handed or gloved. The job: log what they saw (result, treatment, feeding, cage and environment care) before moving to the next animal, plan upcoming checks, and bill for the work.

## Product Purpose
Replace paper records for animal health checks, inspection scheduling and invoicing. Success is a check logged in under a minute, a schedule that is glanceable, and invoices that never get lost between unpaid and paid.

## Brand Personality
Cheerful, sturdy, hands-on. Playful like a sticker book, never childish. The animals are the heroes: each species has its own sticker and color that follows it through the app. Copy is warm, short, Bahasa Indonesia first.

## Anti-references
- Generic Material demo apps (purple seed color, default cards, floating logout buttons).
- Hospital/clinic sterile blue-white dashboards.
- Pastel "kids app" softness that washes out in sunlight.
- Screens drenched edge-to-edge in brand red.

## Design Principles
1. Outdoors first: thick outlines, high contrast, big tap targets; everything must read in direct sun.
2. The animal leads: species identity (sticker + color) is the primary way to scan lists.
3. Tactile, not decorative: motion is a press, a stamp, a state change, never choreography.
4. One vocabulary: the same sticker card, button and field everywhere.
5. Read views read, edit views edit: never show stored data inside editable-looking inputs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA contrast on all text (verified per token). Minimum 48dp touch targets. Respect the OS reduce-motion setting. Status is never color-only (paid/unpaid always carries a label).
